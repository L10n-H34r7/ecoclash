## 📚 Documents

> 🦪 **Pearl** : [Tous les livres de Gardner Dexter (Hiscox)](https://archive.org/search.php?query=creator%3A%22Hiscox%2C+Gardner+Dexter%2C+1822%3F-1908%22) — Mécanique, chimie, moteur à air, à vapeur, à gaz… !!!

- [**Henley's twentieth century formulas**](https://archive.org/details/henleystwentieth00hiscrich), recipes and processes, containing ten thousand selected household and workshop formulas, recipes, processes and moneymaking methods for the practical use of manufacturers, mechanics, housekeepers and home workers.
- [**Mechanical movements, powers, devices and appliances**](https://archive.org/details/mechanicalmovem04hiscgoog) ; by Hiscox, Gardner Dexter, 1822–1908.
- [**Mechanical appliances, mechanical movements and novelties of construction**](https://archive.org/details/mechanicalappli00dextgoog) ; a complete work and a continuation, as a second volume, of the author's book entitled *"Mechanical movements, powers and devices"* … including an explanatory chapter on the leading conceptions of perpetual motion existing during the past three centuries. by Hiscox, Gardner Dexter, 1822–1908.
- [**Receipts and remedies**](https://archive.org/details/receiptsandreme00flemgoog) ; useful hints for everyone on health, beauty, clothing, food. The housewife's complete handbook.
- [**The engineer's sketch-book of mechanical movements, devices, appliances**](https://archive.org/details/engineerssketchb00barb), contrivances and details employed in the design and construction of machinery for every purpose.
- [**Five hundred and seven mechanical movements**](https://archive.org/details/fivehundredseven02brow), embracing all those which are most important in dynamics, hydraulics, hydrostatics, pneumatics, steam engines, mill and other gearing ..  by Brown, Henry T.
- [**The Dangerous Book for Boys**](http://kat.cr/the-dangerous-book-for-boys-us-by-conn-and-hal-iggulden-retail-dwg-t9511544.html) — *« The bestselling book for every boy from eight to eighty, covering essential boyhood skills such as building tree houses, learning how to fish, finding true north. »*
- [**Backyard Ballistics**](http://kat.cr/backyard-ballistics-epub-retail-32skulls-t9572218.html) — *« This bestselling DIY handbook now features new and expanded projects, enabling ordinary folks to construct 16 awesome ballistic devices in their garage or basement workshops using inexpensive household or hardware store materials and this step-by-step guide. »*
- [**Absinthe and Flamethrowers**](http://kat.cr/william-gurstelle-absinthe-and-flamethrowers-projects-and-ruminations-on-the-art-of-living-dangerously-epub-plex-t10379851.html) — *« Projects and Ruminations on the Art of Living Dangerously. »*
- [**Scientific American handy book of facts and formulae**](https://archive.org/details/scientificameric03hopk) by Hopkins, Albert A. (Albert Allis), 1869–1939, ed.
- [**Encyclopedia of practical receipts and processes**](https://archive.org/details/encyclopediaofpr00dickrich). *Containing over 6400 receipts; embracing thorough information, in plain language, applicable to almost every possible industrial and domestic requirement.* by Dick, William B. (William Brisbane), 1827–1901.
- [**Magic; stage illusions and scientific diversions**](https://archive.org/details/magicstageillusi00hopk), including trick photography. by Hopkins, Albert A. (Albert Allis), 1869–1939; Evans, Henry Ridgely, b. 1861. Published 1897.
- [**Uncle Josh's trunk-full of fun**](https://archive.org/details/unclejoshstrunkf00dick) : *« A portfolio of first-class wit and humor, and never-ending source of jollity. »* by Dick, William B. (William Brisbane), 1827–1901; Dick & Fitzgerald. Published 1869.
- [**What shall we do to-night?**](https://archive.org/details/whatshallwedoton00dickrich) or, *Social amusements for evening parties. Furnishing complete and varied programmes for twenty-six entertainments.* by Dick, William B. (William Brisbane), 1827–1901.
- [**Bulletins d'arboriculture, de culture potagère et de floriculture**](https://archive.org/details/bulletinsdarbor04unkngoog) — Publisher Impr. C. Annoot-Braeckman., 1874. Pages 431.
